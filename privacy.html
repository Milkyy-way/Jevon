Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Raise the Vibes respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you submit a form on our website and how we use that information.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact or lead form, we may collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Your area of interest, such as coaching, workshops, speaking, facilitation, or digital programs
  • Basic marketing attribution information, such as the campaign or source that brought you to the page

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Contact you about Raise the Vibes services and opportunities relevant to your request
  • Help you schedule or prepare for a discovery call
  • Understand which marketing campaigns are generating inquiries
  • Improve our website, services, and communications

How We Store and Share Information

Form submissions may be stored using service providers that help us operate the website and manage inquiries, such as Google services. We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate these services or when required by law.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep submitted information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, and support our relationship with you.

Your Choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information you previously submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you. You may make that request through the contact methods available on the Raise the Vibes website.

Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party services such as scheduling platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website and services change. The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated date.

By submitting information through our website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy and understand how the information you provide may be used.
