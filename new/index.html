About Coach Jevon

Your goals. My expertise. Your program.

I'm Jevon Brown-Simpson, a Certified Personal Trainer and performance coach with a background in competitive athletics.

As a former college football player and team captain, I've experienced what it means to train for performance, compete at a high level, and use training to become more capable.

Today, I bring that experience into my coaching.

My approach is centered around resistance training, strength development, muscle building, body recomposition, functional strength, power, and performance.

I don't believe in random workouts or one-size-fits-all programs.

Your training should have a purpose.

Whether you're an athlete, preparing for a demanding career, training for an event, or simply ready to take your fitness seriously, I'll help you build a program that fits your goals, your experience, and your life.

The goal is personal. The training is individualized. The purpose is to build a stronger, more capable body.
