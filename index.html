About Jevon Brown-Simpson

This is more than motivation. It is identity work with direction.

Jevon helps former athletes and young adults to navigate the space between who they were and who they are becoming. Through transformational coaching, speaking, and structured frameworks, he helps people rebuild confidence, clarify purpose, and move forward with aligned action.

  • Built for people facing pressure, transition, uncertainty, or loss of identity after performance-driven seasons of life.
  • Combines lived experience, mindset coaching, and practical tools that move people from internal conflict to forward momentum.
  • Designed for schools, organizations, teams, and individuals who want lasting transformation, not temporary inspiration.
